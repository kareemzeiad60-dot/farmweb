ABOUT FIELDWISE

A simple workspace for running your farm

Fieldwise keeps your fields, crops, bees, animals, machines, money and people in one place, so the whole team works from the same records. It is designed with farms in Egypt’s Nile Delta in mind and works anywhere.

Crops

Fields, crops and planting seasons.

Apiary

Hives, colonies, inspections and honey harvests.

Livestock

Animals, groups, grazing fields and feed mixtures.

Equipment

Tractors, machinery and implements with warranty and service tracking.

Finance

Income and expenses linked to the work behind them.

Team

Invite managers, workers and viewers; assign tasks and track leave.

Weather & holidays

Local weather and public holidays for your region.

Your data, your choice

Keep records in your browser only, or sign in to sync and share them.

Fieldwise is a record-keeping and planning tool. Its forecasts, reminders and summaries are there to help you, not to replace your own judgement or professional advice.

PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

This policy explains what information Fieldwise handles, why, and who can see it. In it, “we” means the operator of this site.

1. Information we handle

  • Account details. Your email address and password when you create a cloud account, plus the farm name you choose at sign-up. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.
  • Farm records you enter. Fields, crops, animals, hives, equipment, financial records, tasks, leave and similar information. This can include names, phone numbers and email addresses of workers.
  • Team information. Members of a farm, their roles, and invitations (the invited email address, role and optional name).
  • Farm profile and location. Your farm’s region, place name and map coordinates, used for weather and public holidays.
  • Local workspace. If you use Fieldwise without signing in, your records stay in your browser’s storage on your device and are not sent to us.

2. How we use it

  • To provide Fieldwise: store your records, show them to the people you share a farm with, and keep farms separate from each other.
  • To show weather and public holidays for your region.
  • To secure the service and respond to support or deletion requests.

We do not sell your information, and we do not show advertising.

3. Who can see your data

  • People in your farm. Everyone you add to a farm can see that farm’s records. Viewers can only read; owners, managers and workers can edit. Only owners and managers can see the member list and invitations.
  • Other farms cannot. Access is restricted per farm in the database.
  • The operator. Whoever administers the database for this site can technically access stored data. They should only do so to run, secure or support the service.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase provides sign-in and the database that stores cloud accounts and farm records.
  • Open-Meteo supplies weather forecasts and place search. Your browser sends it your farm’s coordinates, or the place name you type, and nothing from your account.
  • Nager.Date supplies public holidays. Your browser sends it a country code and year.
  • jsDelivr delivers the Supabase client library that Fieldwise loads.
  • The web host that serves this site receives your IP address and request details, as any web server does.

These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Fieldwise does not use advertising or tracking cookies and has no analytics. It uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in, remember your selected farm, farm profile and region, and hold the local workspace. Clearing site data in your browser removes these items.

6. How long we keep data

Cloud data is kept while your account and farm exist. Deleting a record removes it from the database; backups, if any, are managed by the database provider. To delete your account and data, contact us using the details below. You can export your records from the app at any time.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and each farm’s data is separated by access rules in the database. Invitations can only be accepted by a confirmed email address. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Your choices

  • Edit or delete your records in the app, and export them.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account.
  • Use the local workspace if you prefer your data to stay on your device.

9. Children

Fieldwise is built for people who run or work on farms and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
